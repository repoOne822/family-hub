Six ways to learn the same Grade 1 characters (12 sets × 10 = 120 字), Pinyin tones, sentences and 6 Tang poems. Each mode adapts: 3 stars → next level, 1 star → easier; 2 perfect rounds in a row → a ⚡ challenge level. A character counts as mastered after 3 more first-try rights than misses across listening, reading and writing.
