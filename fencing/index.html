🦉
Coach Hoot: Is he really attacking, or just pretending? Watch his body. Watch for the 💨: on a real attack his back foot pushes and his whole body leans in. A fake is just a wiggle of his arm, and then he pulls back.

🔁 Between-Touch Reset

After EVERY touch, win or lose, do these 4 steps while you walk back to the line. It wipes the last touch away.

  1. 🔄 Turn away: back to your opponent, the last touch is over.
  2. 🌬️ One big breath: in for 4, out slowly.
  3. 🗣️ Say your word: "Next touch", quietly to yourself.
  4. 🎯 Pick ONE plan: what did they just do? What will I try?

🏆 Tournament Day Game Plan

  • 🌙 Night before: pack your bag, then fill in your Pre-Bout Plan Card. Sleep is part of training.
  • 🧦 Warm-up: legs moving, a few lunges, then 3 calm breaths.
  • 🧍 Before each bout: watch your next opponent for 1 minute. Do they attack a lot, retreat, or wait?
  • ⚔️ During the bout: Between-Touch Reset after every touch. Behind on the score? It's just "one touch at a time".
  • 📝 After each bout: do a Real Bout Check below (30 seconds). Win or lose, you get Brave points for checking in.
  • 🤗 After the day: tell Mom or Dad one thing you're proud of. The result is not the whole story.
