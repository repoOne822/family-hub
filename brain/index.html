🧪 Brain Check history

Brain Check every (optional — it never blocks games)
8 subtests at Grade 1–2 level: Reasoning (pattern grids), Memory (numbers forwards & backwards), Speed (30 s symbol search), Word smarts (analogies, odd one out), Number sense (series, mental math); EQ: Reading feelings, Understanding others (false-belief and intention stories), Good choices (self-control). Scores are % of each subtest — progress trends, not a clinical IQ.

⚡ Challenge levels: After 2 rounds in a row with 3 stars, the next round of that game is played one level higher. Beat it (2+ stars) and he jumps ahead (3 stars = 2 levels) with +10 coins; if not, his level stays put — no step down.

Mix it up:
Each kind of brain game (memory, brain builders, puzzles) rests after N rounds in a row until he plays 2 rounds of other kinds. Each game max 4 rounds a day. Art & music cost a 🎟️ ticket, earned every 2 brain games (1 free each day). The kind he has played least today glows ⭐ for +5 coins.

Voice style:

The natural voice is a real neural voice that runs on this device (no account needed). It downloads about 90 MB the first time, then works from the cache.

Device voice:
For the most human-sounding voice on an iPad: Settings → Accessibility → Spoken Content → Voices → English → download a voice marked "Premium" or "Enhanced" (e.g. Ava or Zoe). It is picked automatically.
